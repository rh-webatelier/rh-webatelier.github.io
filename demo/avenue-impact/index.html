What changed in this concept

Same company, same facts. The site now speaks to one buyer and proves its case faster.

  1. One audience. Written for the organisations that buy delivery teams. Learners are pointed to Learning Hub, Mentiiv and PrepnHire instead of sharing the same pages.
  2. The lead offer comes first. The headline sells the managed delivery team and the 14-day deployment, not a general list of capabilities.
  3. Proof before promises. Named clients, their own words, and results written as headlines.
  4. Services and sectors are separated. No placeholders, broken links or internal notes on show.
  5. A brand idea. Avenue means route: the talent pathway from Identify to Measure becomes the visual signature.
